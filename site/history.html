Does it hold up?

Year-over-year correlation of DAWG+ with itself. A metric that measures a real trait should say roughly the same thing about a player twelve months later. Raw clutch metrics famously do not, which is the bar this is trying to clear.

Pooled across every club and season in the window. Regular-season record is the sanity check; the postseason columns are the actual question. Note FIGHT uses Pythagorean expectation from runs rather than real W-L, so these are not circular.

Careers

Mean DAWG+ across the window, with the standard deviation beside it. A low spread with a high mean is the profile the metric is looking for: a player who is like this every year, not one who had a season.
