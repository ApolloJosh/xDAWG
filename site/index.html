The four pillars — BITE (holds his approach under pressure) · GRIT (plays hard, stays available) · HUNT (makes the play that wins it) · FIGHT (shows up against the teams that matter).

DAWG+ compares every player to the league, so raw ability stays in the number. wDAWG+ compares each player to his own norms, so ability cancels out and only change-under-pressure survives — a good hitter who is merely consistent scores near 100 on it. Both are rate stats where 100 is average and 25 points is about one standard deviation.

DAWG and wDAWG are the cumulative versions of each, scaled by playing time, where 0 is league average rather than replacement level. Pillar values are shown as standard deviations from the mean.
